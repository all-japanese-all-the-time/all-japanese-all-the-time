What Is the Science Fiction Sentence Pack?

Do you dream of being able to easily read, understand and enjoy technical manuals, research papers, books and other scientific and technical materials in Japanese? Do you want to be able to talk about more than cherry blossoms and how many siblings you have?

Well, dream no more.

Drawing from a broad range of source materials and specialties, the Science Fiction Sentence Pack has been designed to help you build a technical “STEM” (science, technology, engineering and mathematics) vocabulary, reinforce your understanding of frequently used patterns, improve your reading comprehension, and just generally get you exposed and accustomed to technical writing…

…And all while actually having fun instead of being bored out of your motherloving mind by painfully dry text.

But wait, doesn’t it say science fiction in the name?

Yes. Yes it does.

And there’s a reason for that.

Science fiction isn’t “real”, but it does use real STEM words, and even a lot of real science, taken to certain extremes of course. Science fiction, in fact, leads and drives real science. Pretty much every scientist, technologist and engineer alive today was inspired by sci-fi in his or her youth. Not only that, but s/he’s probably working on bringing what is or used to be a sci-fi concept to life. Best of all, science fiction is fun and interesting in the way all fiction is — it’s full of humor, extremes, powerful imagery and emotions, and that makes it the ideal source material to learn from.

Stuffy traditionalists, that is, people who insist that learning Japanese has to be hard, boring and sadomasochistic, may laugh at your love of the stars — Stargate, Star Trek, Star Wars (lol).

They’ll scoff at the idea of using pop culture to learn a language. They’ll insist in can’t be done.

But they’re wrong. They’ve always been wrong and they’ll always be wrong; after all, they’re the reason why people still think learning Japanese is hard (even though it isn’t): everything’s hard if you do it the hard way. And they’ll be laughing on the other sides of their faces when you know how to say “infrared”, “ultraviolet” and “cosmological singularity” in Japanese, and they don’t.

The key word is “context”. You can’t just get a list of Japanese technical vocab and expect to memorize that shiz and be able to use it properly. That ain’t how it works. You need context. You need the context of how the word is used (so that you can both memorize it easily and learn to use it correctly), and you need the context of fun and wide-eyed-wonderment to keep yourself motivated, engaged, and, well, awake, goldarnit!

So, yeah…that’s what the Science Fiction Sentence Pack is. Technical and scientific vocabulary in the context of awesome action/sci-fi movie lines! Get yours now! 😉
