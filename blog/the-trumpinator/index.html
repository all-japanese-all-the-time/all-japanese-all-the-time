The Trumpinator

Donald. The Donald. Donaldo. Donald John. Donald Johann Trumpinsky III. The DJ. Donald John Trump. Juan Donaldhino do Trumpo.
The man. The legend. The manly legend. The one-man legend.
The man who single-handedly stood up to the tyrannical Barack Kenyatta Obama and exposed him for the Kenyan atheist Muslim Jewish Christian Nazi socialist fascist globalist anti-Semitic politically correct reincarnated pharaoh dictator he is. No one else wanted freedom in America. No one else had the courage. Only Donnie. But what does any of this have to do with Japanese?

Everything.

Let the explanation begin.

Hate him or hate him, you know you love him.
He makes you laugh.
He makes you cry.
He makes you gasp.
He makes you FEEL again.

Donald Trump has turned American presidential politics into a reality TV show. And just like a real reality TV show, watching him is your guilty pleasure. Yeah, you make fun of him, but mockery is the sincerest form of flattery. And the fact that you’re mocking him simply underscores the fact that you’re watching Him, following Him, thinking about Him.

That’s right. Capital letters for pronouns on the Trumpinator. That’s how we roll.

In the morning, you say His name. In the evening, as you fall asleep, you hope to dream of touching His long, flowing, silky, orangutan-orange hair.

OK, no more caps. But still…

You want to be his apprentice. You want him to think big and kick your a$$. You want him to show you the art of dealing with a nation. You want him to fire the political establishment and build a wall with a door to your heart.

We’re running out of puns and references based on Trump book titles here.

Wait, nope, still good. Yeah, so he makes you feel things you’ve never felt before.
You want to be his Ivanka and Ivana. You want him to be leader of your free world. You want him to check your birth certificate.

Which…kind of makes you weird. Why are you so into Donald Trump? Are you some kind of stalker? A Trump stalker? A luxury condo developer stalker? A lux con dev stalker?

Anyway, whatever, you weirdo. You have a Trump addiction. And you want your Trump fix. And now you can have it! You can have it all — in Japanese!

It’s Trumpinator: The Donald Trump Craziness Japanese Sentence Pack!
Coming in like a wrecking ball turn the awesome factor in your Japanese linguistic life right up to 11. Because someone had to do it. So we did it for you. And it won’t cost you billions of dollars or a presidency or national notoriety to get!
And best of all, it’s got no fat, carbs, calories or gluten — just the way you like it! *Cough* eating disorder *cough*.
So all in all it’s, as they used to say in the KGB (now FSB), a pretty good get.

Unfortunately, Donald Trump is married (for now) so you can’t have carnal knowledge of him. But you can have verbal knowledge of him instead. Which, ultimately, is the simpler and emotionally healthier choice. You need to be more circumspect in your relationship choices, and not just boink people because you like their speeches or politics.

Look. Don’t lie. Don’t lie to us. Don’t lie to yourself. You follow the Trump like German stepchildren follow colored pebbles through the forest in the dark. And the guilt is gettin’ to ya. Well, guilt no more, because now you can get your Trump fix without dumbing yourself down to the level of the media. You can actually use your Trumpitis syndrome, your Trump addiction, to help you level up in your other true love — Japanese.

Like, we get it. It’s not necessarily that you want to watch Trump all day every day, it’s that you can’t look away. It’s kind of like that massively-bosomed naked lady we (the Khatzumoto crew) saw one time out ambling nonchalantly along the beslopen streets of San Francisco a few summers ago. We couldn’t look away. We had to look. For science. And science was pleased. And it was good.

Well, no, it was gross because…girls…ew. Traditional values. Traditional values. Traditional values.

Anyway! You have to follow this Trump thing and see how far it goes, right? But from now on, you don’t have to sacrifice your Japanese to make it happen.

Morbid curiosity is a harsh mistress. This whole deal is like a cross between fireworks and a political car accident happening in slow motion every day. It’s like a freight train plowing through the sclerotic B.S. of politics-as-usual, indiscriminately leaving dead bodies in its wake. And you’re rubbernecking. Well, now you can rubberneck in and through Japanese.

Trump is pure emotion and he elicits raw emotion. And that, for better or worse, makes him a language-learning goldmine. These are the facts on the ground. The only bad/out-of-bounds topic in language is the one that bores you. Trump is many things, but he is not boring.

  • What’s Japan saying about Trump?
  • How would you say all those Trumpisms in Japanese?
  • What would be fun to read in Japanese?
  • What’s a great way to learn and have fun at the same time?
  • How might one go about indulging one’s basest, tabloidiest curiosities while also enriching one’s mind?
  • What would it be like to learn real, grown-up, serious newspaper vocabulary in Japanese but with all the pleasurable guilt and giggles of reading comedy, smut and trash?
  • How would you express your love/hate of, lust/derision/loathing/support/disgust/respect (grudging or otherwise) for, and/or apathy towards Trump — and by extension anyone and anything else — in Japanese?

The answers to all these questions and more are contained in the Trumpinator: The Donald Trump Madness Japanese Sentence Pack. What’s that, you say? It was called “Craziness” before? Well, it’s called Madness now. What’re you gonna do, make us apologize in public to some husky-voiced blonde newscaster on Fox News? We’ll build a wall around your house, dude! A big, beautiful wall. ‘Cause we’re builders. And then we’ll take you down to Fifth Avenue and shoot you!

Oh, wait, one more thing. This Trump Sentence Pack isn’t for everyone. You need to fulfill the following three requirements to qualify for the privilege of owning it:

  1. Know hiragana and katakana down pat. Like completely. No ifs, ands or buts. No romaji bandits allowed.
  2. Know at least 100 kanji (and preferably 500-1000+). No kanji virgins allowed.
  3. Be a (closeted/in-denial) Trump lover. Having come this far, we’re pretty sure you’re one of those already.

Also, the Trump himself swears from time to time (a heckuva lot for a non-Australian politician speaking on record) and people get a bit raw and passionate about politics these days, so there is occasional — but not gratuitous — profanity and graphic language amidst the witty barbs, banter and political jargon of the Trump Sentence Pack. Not Quentin Tarantino/Peter Capaldi/John Hannah levels by any means, but, yeah…it comes up. So, being comfortable with that is also a requirement of sorts.

Speaking of comfort: you get to choose how you react to your new overlord, Dear Leader and President-for-Life Trump. You can either sit around and be outraged at the Right/the Left/the Center/foreigners/Americans/everyone/everything like the media and every candidate (regardless of their hair prosthesis status) wants you to, or you can act like a free human being and use your outrage to do something constructive, like get good at Japanese. All politics is a game and you are being played. You can either play to win or sit around watching/going to rallies and counter-rallies. You can be a pawn in someone else’s grand chessgame, or you can play your own game entirely. What you cannot laugh at controls you. Stew in righteous indignation like a Knorr beef cube or come be free. Your call, playa.
